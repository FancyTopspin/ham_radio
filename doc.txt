Ham Radio License Exam Web App: Architecture & System Blueprint
Overview
A modern, scalable study platform for amateur radio exam preparation (Technician, General, and Amateur Extra), designed for zero-friction onboarding via a hybrid local-first architecture coupled with a robust Django backend.

Detailed Component Specifications
1. Data Model & Versioning
* Composite Versioning (pool_version + question_code): Ensures updates across the NCVEC 4-year cycles (Technician 2026–2030, General 2027, Extra 2028) maintain historical accuracy.
* Option Shuffling: Answer choices (A, B, C, D) are randomized on the client side during rendering to prevent positional memorization; database tracks choice IDs rather than static letter strings.
2. Sync & Event Log Architecture
* Local-First with Optional Cloud Sync (Option C): Users can study immediately without creating an account.
* Append-Only Attempt History: Every interaction (study answer or practice exam attempt) is logged with an immutable UUID, timestamp, selected choice ID, and mode (study or practice_exam).
* Conflict-Free Sync: When signing in or syncing, unsynced local events are pushed to Django in batch and inserted with conflict ignoring, enabling comprehensive longitudinal progress reports.
3. Study & Spaced Repetition Engine (Hybrid Option C)
* Continuous Weighted Queue: Combines dynamic bucket weights (Unseen, Needs Work, Learning, Mastered) to allow infinite self-paced cramming without blocking users.
* Time-Decay Multiplier: Automatically reintroduces Mastered questions into the review queue if they haven't been practiced within a specified time window.
4. Exam Simulator & Analytics Engine
* Blueprint-Weighted Mock Exams: Strictly enforces official NCVEC sub-element quotas (e.g., 35 questions for Tech/General requiring 26/35 correct; 50 questions for Extra requiring 37/50 correct).
* Targeted "Drill My Mistakes" Mode: Aggregates failed attempts from past exams and study sessions into a one-click rescue mode targeting exact weak spots.
